About The Event

The 5th International Conference on Advanced Computational and Communication Paradigms (ICACCP-2025) will be held from June 6 to June 8, 2025, at SMIT, Sikkim, India. ICACCP, which began in 2017, has been conducted once in two years. The upcoming edition will cover various aspects of algorithmic and communication paradigms, including but not limited to advanced computation, AI applications, blockchain and cybersecurity, the Internet of Things, and more.

Publications of ICACCP are handled by Springer and previous volumes are available at the following links: Volume 3, Volume 2, and Volume 1. Researchers worldwide are invited to submit original, high-quality research papers.

Event Venue

Event Venue Location

Sikkim Manipal Institute of Technlogy, Majhitar

The Sikkim Manipal Institute of Technology (SMIT), a constituent college of Sikkim Manipal University, is located on the banks of the river Teesta, amidst lush green hills in the state of Sikkim, India. Established in 1997, SMIT is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).


The institute earned the distinction of prestigious accreditation for its engineering disciplines from the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) via ACCR-860/06, dated January 22, 2008. Additionally, SMIT holds ISO 9001 certification (NS-EN ISO 9001:2008) for all its courses.


The engineering college has its own dedicated campus at Majitar, Rangpo, in East Sikkim. The campus is safe, secure, and offers an excellent academic environment.


The Department of Computer Science and Engineering offers five programs: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in CSE, Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in CSE (AI & ML), Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in CSE (IoT & Cybersecurity including Blockchain Technology), Master of Technology (M.Tech.) in CSE, and Ph.D. The department has an AICTE-approved intake of 300 students for the B.Tech. program and 18 students for the M.Tech. program. With these programs, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering serves over 1000 students.
